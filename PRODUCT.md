## Product Purpose
Sanctum is a private members-only platform for intellectual seekers studying Hermeticism, AI, philosophy, astrology, and mental sovereignty. It combines deep-learning courses (trilhas), community (Ordem), live events, and gamification — a sacred space where knowledge acquisition is treated as a transformative ritual, not passive consumption.

## Users
Primary: autodidact adults (25–45), Portuguese-speaking, serious learners who rejected mainstream education for self-directed mastery. They are tech-comfortable, aesthetically literate, and allergic to anything that feels corporate or generic. They open Sanctum the way someone opens a private journal — with intention and focus.

Secondary: moderators and the Grão-Mestre (admin), who tend the community and publish content.

## Brand Voice
Precise, purposeful, slightly austere. Sanctum does not perform mysticism — it embodies depth. Every word earns its place. Vocabulary: "trilha" (course), "Ordem" (community), "espaço" (subforum), "Iniciado / Aprendiz / Adepto / Mestre / Grão-Mestre" (levels).

## Anti-references
- Netflix (too mainstream, too saturated, pure entertainment)
- Typical SaaS dark dashboards (too cold, too corporate, no soul)
- Crypto/web3 platforms (neon purple, garish gold, excess glow)
- Generic LMS platforms (Udemy, Hotmart defaults — flat, bright, transactional)
- Mysticism clichés (stars/constellations on every surface, pentagram decorations, gradient halos)

## Core Principles
- Private, not public. The interface reinforces that you earned your way in.
- Knowledge over entertainment. Dense, serious, intentional.
- Timeless over trendy. Visual choices should feel correct in five years.
- The tool should disappear into the task.
